The Challenge

Farming Decisions Are Still Too Fragmented

Farmers today rely on scattered, disconnected sources for critical decisions. Information about soil, crops, weather, markets, and government schemes exists in silos — making it harder to plan, grow, and profit confidently.

Fragmented Information

Farming advice, market data, weather, and government schemes are spread across dozens of disconnected sources.

Complex Soil Data

Soil health reports are technical and hard to interpret, leaving farmers unsure about what to do with the results.

Crop Selection Uncertainty

Choosing the right crop requires understanding soil, season, water, and market conditions — too much guesswork.

No Complete Guidance

Step-by-step farming guidance from sowing to harvesting is rarely available in one accessible place.

Unclear Mandi Prices

Real-time market prices are hard to access, making it difficult to sell at the right time and right place.

Weather Risk

Unexpected weather events can devastate crops, and timely, location-specific alerts are often missing.

Weak Record-Keeping

Most farmers have no easy digital way to track expenses, yields, activities, or farm history for future decisions.

Limited Resource Access

Finding available machinery, leasing land, or discovering relevant government schemes remains a manual struggle.

KisanSangam brings it all together — one platform that connects every part of the farming journey with intelligence, guidance, and practical tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Getting Started

Can I manage more than one farm?

Yes. KisanSangam supports multiple farm profiles. You can add each plot with its distinct location, soil type, and water source to receive specific recommendations for each.

Do I need to book a soil test first?

No. You do not need to book a soil test to use the app. You can get started right away using your known soil information or manual inputs. An integrated soil testing service is coming soon.

Where can I get help?

You can reach our support team directly by emailing manavpatel.cloud@gmail.com or messaging us on Instagram.

Download Questions

Download & Compatibility FAQs

How do I download KisanSangam?

Simply click the "Download APK" button on this page. The APK file will download directly to your Android device without requiring sign-up or registration.

Which Android versions are supported?

KisanSangam supports Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above (API level 26 or higher). Your device must meet the minimum Android version shown above and have sufficient free storage to download and install the app.

Can I install it on an iPhone?

Currently, this APK release is available only for Android devices. An iOS version is not available at this time.

Why does Android ask for installation permission?

When installing an app outside the Google Play Store, Android prompts you to verify permission for the browser you used to download the file. This is standard Android security.

What should I do if installation fails?

Check that the download completed, your device meets the listed Android requirement, and sufficient storage is available. If installation still fails, contact our support team with the error message at manavpatel.cloud@gmail.com.

Is Soil Testing available in the app?

Integrated Soil Testing is Coming Soon. You can currently enter your known soil data (pH, soil type, nutrients) to receive tailored crop guidance.
