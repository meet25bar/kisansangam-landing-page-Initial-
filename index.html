The Challenge

Farming Decisions Are Still Too Fragmented

Farmers today rely on scattered, disconnected sources for critical decisions. Information about soil, crops, weather, markets, and government schemes exists in silos — making it harder to plan, grow, and profit confidently.

Fragmented Information

Farming advice, market data, weather, and government schemes are spread across dozens of disconnected sources.

Complex Soil Data

Soil health reports are technical and hard to interpret, leaving farmers unsure about what to do with the results.

Crop Selection Uncertainty

Choosing the right crop requires understanding soil, season, water, and market conditions — too much guesswork.

No Complete Guidance

Step-by-step farming guidance from sowing to harvesting is rarely available in one accessible place.

Unclear Mandi Prices

Real-time market prices are hard to access, making it difficult to sell at the right time and right place.

Weather Risk

Unexpected weather events can devastate crops, and timely, location-specific alerts are often missing.

Weak Record-Keeping

Most farmers have no easy digital way to track expenses, yields, activities, or farm history for future decisions.

Limited Resource Access

Finding available machinery, leasing land, or discovering relevant government schemes remains a manual struggle.

KisanSangam brings it all together — one platform that connects every part of the farming journey with intelligence, guidance, and practical tools.

Soil Understanding Made Simple

Soil health is the foundation of good farming. KisanSangam helps you understand your soil's nutrient levels, pH, texture, and provides clear recommendations — not just raw lab numbers.
