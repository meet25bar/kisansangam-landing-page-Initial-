How It Connects

One farm. Connected decisions.

Your farm profile and soil information support crop selection. The selected crop leads into a practical farm guide. Weather, task reminders and market information help throughout the season.

      Organic Farming

      A dedicated path for organic farming.

      Crop recommendations and farm guidance for cultivation without synthetic chemical fertilizers or pesticides.

      What organic support means

      KisanSangam's organic farming support provides crop recommendations and stage-by-stage farm guidance suitable for organic cultivation — focusing on soil health, organic nutrient sources and crop-care practices that do not rely on synthetic chemical fertilizers or synthetic pesticides.

      Availability of organic guidance depends on the crop and locally supported information. Organic guidance does not certify a farm or its produce. KisanSangam does not imply guaranteed organic premiums or certification eligibility.

      How to access organic guidance

      1. Download the KisanSangam Android app from our download page.
      2. Set up your farm profile with your location and soil information.
      3. Select organic farming as your approach when choosing a crop.
      4. Receive recommendations and guidance suited to organic cultivation for supported crops.
      Get Started

      How KisanSangam Works

      From downloading the app to using it through the farming season — here's what to expect.

      1

      Download the Android App

      Download the KisanSangam APK from our download page and install it on your Android device. Follow the installation instructions carefully.

      2

      Set Up Your Farm Profile

      Enter your farm location, soil information and water availability. The more you share, the more relevant your recommendations will be — but you can start with what you have.

      3

      Get Crop Recommendations

      Compare suitable crops for your farm with clear reasoning for each option. Select the crop that fits your season and plans.

      4

      Follow Your Farm Guide

      Access your stage-by-stage cultivation guide. Return to it throughout the season for activity reminders, timing guidance and crop care instructions.

      5

      Track Weather & Markets

      Stay updated with location-specific weather forecasts and the latest available mandi prices to help you plan your activities and selling decisions.

      6

      Manage, Diagnose & Plan

      Use the AI Farming Assistant for crop problem guidance, keep digital farm records, estimate costs and profits, and explore government scheme information — all in one place.
